使用帮助

三步给旧照片加上摄影风格，之后在「照片」App 中继续调整。

三步开始

  1. 选择照片：在首页轻点「选择照片」从图库选择，或「文件 · RAW」导入 HEIC、JPG、RAW 与实况照片（HEIC + MOV）。一次最多 30 张。
  2. 添加风格并存入：单张照片在工作台轻点「添加风格并存入照片」；多张照片在首页轻点「全部添加」，完成后轻点「存入照片 · N 张」。App 在本机检查兼容性，写入并校验风格数据。
  3. 到「照片」App 编辑：存入后轻点「打开照片 App」，选中照片进入「编辑」，即可使用摄影风格。

照片工作台

在首页轻点一张照片，打开工作台，存入之前先看看效果。

  • 编辑：进入质感与调色。质感有柔肤、光晕、胶片，颗粒可单独叠加；调色有暖陶、麦穗等 App 预设，可调明暗与色彩。「恢复」还原全部调整，「完成」回到工作台。
  • 对比：轻点「对比」后拖动分界线，左右比较原图与效果。
  • 按住看原图：按住按钮，随时查看未调整的原图。
  • 双指放大：双指张开，照片浮出面板查看细节，松手复原。

调色免费；质感与颗粒为终身会员功能，可先预览。苹果官方摄影风格请在「照片」App 中编辑。

一次存入

摄影风格、「照片」App 质感与 App 内调整在同一次保存中写入，校验通过后才显示成功。

  • 修改原照片：直接更新图库中的这张照片，可随时在「照片」中「编辑 > 复原」。
  • 存为新照片：另外新增一张，原照片不变。从「文件」导入、已编辑过的照片和实况照片只能这样存，存入前会说明原因。

常见问题

原照片会被删除或覆盖吗？

不会。「存为新照片」会另外新增一张；「修改原照片」使用系统的可复原编辑，随时可在「照片」中「编辑 > 复原」。从「文件」导入的原文件也不会被改动。

哪些照片可以添加摄影风格？

主要面向 iPhone 15 及更早机型拍摄的 HEIC 照片，以及相机拍摄的 JPG / RAW。相机 JPG 会先转为 HEIC 再添加风格，可在「设置」中调整 HEIC 画质。能否在「照片」中编辑摄影风格，取决于照片、机型和系统版本。

「照片」App 质感需要什么条件？

写入「照片」App 的质感、颗粒与光晕需要 iOS 27 或更高版本，可在「设置 > 摄影风格」中开关。它与 App 内的质感效果是两项不同的功能。

实况照片支持吗？

支持。照片与视频一起添加摄影风格和 App 内效果，存为新的实况照片，原实况照片及其视频保留。在工作台中可以播放预览效果，或切换查看原视频。

保存时提示 3302 或「资源无效」？

这是系统拒绝了写回原照片。请改用「存为新照片」，原照片保持不变。

免费额度和会员是怎样的？

可免费成功生成并校验 10 张风格照片，导入失败不计数，同一结果保存失败后的重试不重复计数。额度只在本机记录，不跨设备同步。一次购买「终身会员」即可不限张数，并解锁质感与颗粒，无订阅。更换设备或重装后，在「设置 > 会员」中轻点「恢复购买」。购买按钮显示当前 App Store 地区的实际价格；其他地区采用 Apple 以人民币 18 元为基准自动生成的价格档位，并非实时汇率报价。

「App 内处理文件」是什么？可以清除吗？

导入时 App 会复制一份原件并保存处理结果，用于重试、导出和检查。它们只在本 App 内，不是「照片」里的照片。在「设置 > 存储空间」可清除已保存或已导出的部分。

RAW 格式都能处理吗？

不能保证。DNG、RW2、ARW 等需要逐个检查系统是否能解码该格式及具体相机型号。不能解码时会显示失败；可从相机导出 JPG 再尝试。

Support

Add Photographic Styles to older photos in three steps, then keep adjusting in Photos.

Getting started

  1. Choose photos. On the home screen, tap Choose Photos to pick from your library, or Files · RAW to import HEIC, JPG, RAW and Live Photos (HEIC + MOV). Up to 30 at a time.
  2. Add style and save. For one photo, tap “Add Style & Save to Photos” in the workbench. For several, tap Add All on the home screen, then “Save to Photos · N”. The app checks compatibility and writes and verifies the style data on device.
  3. Edit in Photos. After saving, tap Open Photos, select the photo and tap Edit to use Photographic Styles.

Photo workbench

Tap a photo on the home screen to open the workbench and preview before you save.

  • Edit opens Texture and Colour. Texture: Soft Skin, Glow and Film, with Grain as a separate layer. Colour: app presets such as Terracotta and Wheat, plus Tone and Colour sliders. Reset undoes all adjustments; Done returns to the workbench.
  • Compare: tap Compare, then drag the divider to compare the original and the effect.
  • Hold for Original: hold the button to see the unadjusted original at any time.
  • Pinch to zoom: spread two fingers to lift the photo over the panel and check details; let go to spring back.

Colour is free; Texture and Grain are Lifetime features you can preview first. Apple’s own Photographic Styles are edited in Photos.

One save

The style, Photos texture and in-app adjustments are written in a single save, and success only shows after verification.

  • Modify Original changes this photo in your library; you can always use Edit > Revert in Photos.
  • Save as New Photo adds a photo and leaves the original unchanged. File imports, already-edited photos and Live Photos can only be saved this way, and the reason is shown before saving.

FAQ

Are my originals kept?

Yes. Saving as new adds a photo; updating the original uses revertible editing (Edit > Revert in Photos). Imported files are never modified.

Which photos work?

Mainly HEIC photos from iPhone 15 and earlier, plus camera JPG / RAW. Camera JPGs are converted to HEIC before the style is added; set the HEIC quality in Settings. Native style editing depends on the photo, device and iOS version.

What does Photos texture need?

Texture, Grain & Glow written for Photos need iOS 27 or later and can be switched in Settings > Photographic Styles. This is separate from the app’s own texture effects.

Live Photos

Supported. The photo and video get the style and in-app effects together and are saved as a new Live Photo; the original Live Photo and its video are kept. In the workbench you can play a preview of the effect or switch to the original video.

Error 3302 or “invalid resource” when saving?

The system refused to write back to the original. Use Save as New Photo instead; the original stays unchanged.

Free photos and the lifetime unlock

10 successfully generated and verified style outputs are free. Failed imports do not count; retrying a failed save of the same output is not charged twice. The local allowance does not sync across devices. A one-time Lifetime purchase removes the limit and unlocks Texture and Grain, with no subscription. Restore ownership in Settings > Membership > Restore Purchases. The purchase button shows the current App Store price for your region. Apple derives regional price points from the CNY 18 base; this is not a live exchange-rate quote.

In-app processing files

On import the app keeps a copy of the original and the results for retries, export and verification. They live only inside the app, not in Photos. Clear the saved or exported ones in Settings > Storage.

RAW support

Each DNG, RW2, ARW or other RAW file is checked against the system decoder for its format and camera model. Universal support is not promised. Export a JPG from the camera if decoding fails.
