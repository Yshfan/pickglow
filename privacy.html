Pickglow 风格

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 16 日

使用帮助English

概要

Pickglow 风格（以下简称「本 App」）不收集、不上传、不出售任何个人信息。所有照片处理都在你的设备上完成。本 App 没有账号系统，不接入广告或第三方统计，也没有自己的服务器。

照片访问

你选择照片后，本 App 读取这些照片的原始文件，并在本机：

  • 检查照片格式与兼容性；
  • 添加摄影风格相关数据，或按你的调整生成新版本；
  • 根据你的选择，存为新照片，或通过系统的可复原编辑更新原照片。

为了重试、导出和校验，本 App 会在自己的沙盒中保存所选照片的副本和处理结果。你可以在「设置 > 存储空间」中清除；删除本 App 也会一并删除这些文件。这些文件不会离开你的设备。

人脸与人物数据

「柔肤」等功能使用 Apple 的 Vision 框架，在本机检测当前照片中的人脸与五官位置，仅用于这一次处理。检测结果不会被上传、汇总或用于识别身份。

购买

终身会员通过 Apple 的 App 内购买完成。付款和交易记录由 Apple 处理，本 App 无法获取你的付款信息。为计算免费额度，本 App 在设备的钥匙串中保存「已处理张数」这一个数字，不与任何身份信息关联，也不会上传。

网络

本 App 只在以下情况联网：从 iCloud 照片下载你所选照片的原件（由系统完成），以及与 App Store 通信以完成购买或恢复购买。

儿童隐私

本 App 不面向特定年龄群体收集信息，也不收集儿童的个人信息。

政策变更与联系

如本政策变更，会更新本页并修改生效日期。如有问题，请通过 GitHub Issues 联系我们。

Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026

Summary

Pickglow Styles (“the app”) does not collect, upload or sell any personal data. All photo processing happens on your device. There are no accounts, no ads, no third-party analytics and no app servers.

Photos

When you choose photos, the app reads their original files and, on device, checks compatibility, adds Photographic Style data or applies your adjustments, then saves a new photo or updates the original through the system’s revertible editing, as you choose. Working copies and results are kept inside the app’s sandbox for retrying, exporting and verification. You can clear them in Settings > Storage, and they are removed with the app. They never leave your device.

Faces

Features such as Soft Skin use Apple’s Vision framework on device to locate faces and facial features in the current photo, only for that edit. Nothing is uploaded, aggregated or used for identification.

Purchases

The lifetime unlock is an Apple In-App Purchase. Payment is handled by Apple; the app never sees your payment details. To track the free allowance, the app stores a single number — how many photos were processed — in the device keychain. It is not linked to you and is never uploaded.

Network

The app goes online only to download originals of photos you choose from iCloud Photos (handled by the system) and to talk to the App Store for purchases and restores.

Contact

Questions: GitHub Issues. Changes to this policy will be posted on this page.
